Why Unwip

Three promises, kept

Private and safe

There’s no Unwip account and no Unwip server, so there’s nothing of yours to leak, sell or train an AI on.

  • Your writing stays on your devices, and in your own cloud (Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or iCloud Drive) only if you choose.
  • Saves are crash-safe: each one is written in full before it replaces the last.
  • Automatic backups and snapshots. If two devices change the same chapter, both versions are kept.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Formatting that travels

Italics, headings, scene breaks, footnotes, comments and fonts look the same wherever you open the book.

  • PC, Mac, iPhone and iPad, kept in step through your own cloud: Dropbox on every device, or OneDrive, Google Drive and iCloud Drive between computers.
  • In and out of Scrivener, Word, Google Docs, Obsidian and Markdown without losing your italics.
  • Pasting keeps the formatting you want and drops the clutter.
  • Fonts are matched across devices (Palatino on your PC is Palatino on your iPhone).

Completely yours

Your books are ordinary files in a folder you choose, not locked inside an account or a cloud.

  • Take the whole book out any time: Scrivener, Word, Markdown, PDF, EPUB or a full backup.
  • Works offline. Nothing needs Unwip to be around tomorrow.
  • No subscription and no lock-in.

A page that stays out of your way

Start with only the manuscript. Your chapters and scenes live in the binder, the page is just the page, and Unwip saves every few seconds and keeps backups.

  • Paste that behaves. From Word, Google Docs or Scrivener, with the stray blank lines tidied.
  • Goals and sprints. A daily word goal, timed sprints, and Spark when you’re stuck.
  • Revise without AI. Repeated words, long sentences and grammar slips, by fixed rules.
  • Snapshots. Save a version before a big rewrite, compare it, bring it back.

Everywhere

Your book on the PC, the Mac, the iPhone

The same book on every device. Keep your books in Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or iCloud Drive and Unwip keeps them in step (on iPhone, iPad and the browser, that’s Dropbox for now); if two devices change the same chapter, it keeps both versions and lets you choose.

  • Windows: the full app, which keeps itself up to date.
  • Mac: the full app, in beta.
  • iPhone and iPad: no App Store needed, straight from Safari to your Home Screen.

Bringing it in

Import a Word file, or a Google Doc downloaded as Word, and Unwip splits it into chapters at your headings, “Chapter” lines or page breaks. You see the chapters before anything is made, and can change how it splits. Several files become several chapters.

Pasting

Paste from Word or Google Docs and your italics and bold stay, while the stray blank lines, odd fonts and spacing are tidied. Want the blank lines back? One button.

Going back

Copy for Docs puts a chapter on the clipboard ready to paste into Google Docs with its formatting. Or export Word files: the standard manuscript format for agents, a beta-reader copy, a file per chapter, or one file for Vellum and Atticus.

Your vault, as a book

Import a folder of chapter files and pick your vault. Sub-folders become folders in the binder, in order, with chapter 2 before chapter 10. Obsidian’s own settings and drawings are left out.

Clean text

Properties, hidden comments, embedded pictures and callout labels are tidied away; [[links]] become plain words, and HTML like <u> or <b> becomes real formatting.

Going back

Export the book as a folder of Markdown files that Obsidian, iA Writer and Bear read the same way, with footnotes and a Pictures folder.

A gentle start

Say what you wrote with before and Unwip sets itself up to feel familiar, then brings your book straight in. An example book is there to poke at: the opening of Bram Stoker’s Dracula, its diary entries and letters laid out as scenes, with characters and a timeline.

A short tour

A quick walk round the binder, the page and the modes, then out of your way. Help is always one key away (F1).

Room to grow

Characters, places and notes beside your manuscript, plus templates for magic systems, factions and more when your world gets bigger.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, while it’s in beta. Pricing, if there is any, hasn’t been decided.

Does it use AI?

No. Nothing in Unwip writes, rewrites or suggests for you. Spelling and grammar checks follow fixed rules and run on your device.

Is my writing private?

Yes. Unwip has no account and no server of its own, so your writing never passes through Unwip. It stays on your devices, and in your own cloud (Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or iCloud Drive) if you connect it. Nothing is used to train AI, and there’s no tracking. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it, such as a problem report you email or a Proof of writing report you share.

Where are my books stored?

On your device, or in your own cloud: Dropbox works everywhere, and on a PC or Mac you can also use OneDrive, Google Drive or iCloud Drive. Unwip doesn’t ask you to sign up, and your writing isn’t uploaded to Unwip.

Will importing change my Scrivener project?

No. Importing makes a new Unwip book, and your Scrivener project stays where it is.

Why does my computer warn me before it opens?

Unwip isn’t registered with Microsoft or Apple yet (that costs a yearly fee). On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. You only do it once.

What is Proof of writing?

An optional record of how your book was written: every change, with the time, kept with the book. You can play it back, share a report that shows what was typed and what was pasted, or make a timelapse video. It shows how the words reached the page; on its own it can’t prove who was typing.

How do I report a problem?

In Unwip, click Report a problem at the bottom of the main screen. It adds the details that help (your version and device, never your writing) and shows you exactly what’s sent. Or post it in the Discord.

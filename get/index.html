Moving from another app?

From Obsidian

Import › A folder of chapter files… and pick your vault. Your folders come across as folders, in order. Obsidian’s own settings, links and markup are tidied away.

From Scrivener

Import › Scrivener project (.scriv)… Chapters, notes, synopses and what’s left out of the compile all come across, and you can send it back to Scrivener any time.

Good to know

“Windows protected your PC”

Windows says this about apps from small makers that haven’t paid for a signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only see it once.

Writing on more than one device

Connect Dropbox in Unwip on each device and your books stay in step. On Windows, keep the book in your Dropbox folder.
